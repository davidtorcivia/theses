Other MCP clients and the REST API {{if .MCPConfig}}

Set the Authorization header to Bearer YOUR_KEY. Never put your key in the URL.

Calendar sync

Follow production milestones, tasks and events in Google Calendar or Apple Calendar/iCloud.

{{if eq .Section "calendar"}}{{template "notice" .}}{{end}}

Includes accessible active episodes, unfinished dated tasks, and Show events with their notes. Dates are all-day events. Change them in Theses; your calendar service decides when updates arrive.

{{if .CalendarURL}}{{end}}
  1. Google Calendar: on a computer, choose Other calendars + → From URL, paste the link, then Add calendar.
  2. Apple Calendar on Mac: choose File → New Calendar Subscription, paste the link, then choose iCloud as the account to see it on your other devices.

Anyone with this private link can read included titles, dates and event notes. Replacing or revoking it stops future access; calendar services may retain earlier copies. Subscribe by URL instead of importing a file to keep receiving changes.
