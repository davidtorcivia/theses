Backups

Every night the database and the markdown mirror go to the bucket under the {{.Backups.Prefix}} prefix, as one archive encrypted with a key derived from THESES_SECRET_KEY: a restore needs that variable and nothing else. Three things are set up on the bucket, because none of them can be done from here. One, an application key restricted to that prefix with write and list but no delete, which is the key below. Two, Object Lock in compliance mode with a retention of the keep window, so nothing, including the account owner, can remove a backup before it expires. Three, server-side encryption on the bucket. Old archives are expired by the bucket's lifecycle rule; nothing here ever deletes one.
